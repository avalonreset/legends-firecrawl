# Why the bypass claim was retired

The original manifesto confused catalog classifications with implemented, reliable substitutes. The percentage-savings claim was unsupported and is withdrawn.

The 0.2.0 decision retires all native bypass routing. The product uses official Firecrawl for execution and adds workflow convenience, bounded jobs and preserved response provenance. It does not promise less expensive equivalent data or superior intelligence.

Managed services can add maintained integration, normalization and enrichment even where source records are public. Free discovery is already part of Firecrawl. Its API and CLI also operate without MCP.

The [historical live audit](../../docs/NATIVE-LIVE-AUDIT-20261001.md) remains useful evidence of what was tried and why broad claims were unwarranted. No historical successful direct request changes the current official-only scope. See [release scope](../../docs/RELEASE-SCOPE.md).
